**Who they are:** The top law enforcement officials in each state, Republicans and Democrats alike, who have become some of the most active AI regulators.

**What they want:** To protect children and consumers in their states. In August 2025, 44 attorneys general warned AI companies they would be held accountable for harms to children. In June 2026 Florida's attorney general sued OpenAI and Sam Altman, the [first state to sue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_artificial_intelligence) over ChatGPT.

**Resources and levers:** Consumer-protection and fraud laws they can enforce without new legislation, subpoenas, and lawsuits. California's and Delaware's attorneys general set conditions on OpenAI's 2025 restructuring because they oversee charities.

**Obstacles and opponents:** AI companies, and federal preemption efforts, which most attorneys general from both parties have opposed.
