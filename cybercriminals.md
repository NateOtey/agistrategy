**Who they are:** Ransomware gangs, fraudsters and lone criminals using AI to scale their work. In 2025 Anthropic [reported](https://www.anthropic.com/news/detecting-countering-misuse-aug-2025) a criminal using Claude Code to run a data-extortion campaign against at least 17 organizations, a practice it called "vibe hacking."

**What they want:** Money, through ransomware, extortion, phishing, romance scams and deepfake impersonation. In one 2024 case, a deepfaked video call with a company's "CFO" tricked an employee at the engineering firm Arup into sending $25 million.

**Resources and levers:** AI that lowers the skill needed to write malware or convincing scams, open-weight models without safeguards, and the global reach of the internet.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Law enforcement, banks, cybersecurity firms, and AI labs that monitor for misuse. Defenders using AI too, which may matter most in the long run.
