**What it is:** A Santa Monica think tank long tied to US national security, with a large AI program. It was influential in shaping the Biden administration's AI policy.

**What it wants:** To give the US government rigorous analysis of AI's security implications, including biological risks, model-weight security and competition with China.

**Resources and levers:** Deep relationships with the Pentagon and intelligence agencies, a reputation for nonpartisan analysis, and widely adopted frameworks, such as its 2024 [security levels for protecting model weights](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA2849-1.html).

**Obstacles and opponents:** Critics on the tech right who see it as too close to the safety movement and its funders. A political environment less receptive to risk-focused policy.
