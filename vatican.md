**Who he is:** The first American pope, elected in May 2025, who said he chose his name partly because of AI: Leo XIII had addressed the industrial revolution in *Rerum Novarum*. His first encyclical, [*Magnifica Humanitas*](https://www.stlouisreview.com/story/pope-leo-xiv-to-publish-artificial-intelligence-encyclical) (May 2026), is about AI.

**What he wants:** AI that serves human dignity, protects workers and families, and helps young people think instead of thinking for them. The Church has called for strong ethical limits, including on autonomous weapons.

**Resources and levers:** Moral authority with 1.4 billion Catholics, including many US politicians, the Vice President among them. Convening power across religions and political divides. A long tradition of social teaching on labor.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Accelerationists who see moral limits as obstacles. Little direct policy power. A media environment that may treat church teaching as one more opinion.
