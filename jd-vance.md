**Who he is:** The Vice President, a former venture capitalist and Thiel protégé, and a likely 2028 presidential candidate.

**What he wants:** At the Paris summit in February 2025, he said "the AI future is not going to be won by hand-wringing about safety" and warned Europe against heavy regulation. He also wants AI that raises workers' wages instead of replacing them, and has said he read [*AI 2027*](https://ai-2027.com) and takes the risk of losing control seriously, while doubting the US could pause without China doing the same.

**Resources and levers:** The vice presidency and its platform, ties to both the tech right and the populist right, and a central role in the Republican coalition's future.

**Obstacles and opponents:** European regulators. Safety advocates who want binding rules. The tension within his own coalition between tech donors and populists worried about jobs.
