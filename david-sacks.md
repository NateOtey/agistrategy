**Who he is:** A venture capitalist (Craft Ventures), All-In podcast host and former PayPal executive. He served as the White House AI and crypto czar until [March 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/26/david-sacks-is-done-as-ai-czar-heres-what-hes-doing-instead/) and now co-chairs the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology.

**What he wants:** Light-touch regulation, a federal standard that overrides the states, and an AI race against China won by getting the world to adopt American chips and models. He has argued that fears of AI catastrophe are overblown and accused Anthropic of running "a sophisticated regulatory capture strategy based on fear-mongering."

**Resources and levers:** A large audience through All-In, influence with the President, and a role in shaping the administration's preemption push and chip-export policy. His new post is advisory, with less direct power than the czar role.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Anthropic, AI safety organizations and their funders. State legislators. China hawks who think his views on chip sales are too permissive. Populists in his own party.
