**What it is:** The reporters and outlets that shape how the public and policymakers understand AI, from *The Information*, Bloomberg and the *New York Times* (including its *Hard Fork* podcast) to newsletters like *Platformer* and *Transformer*.

**What it wants:** Scoops, audience and accountability. Some coverage leans toward hype, some toward skepticism; most of it depends on access to the companies it covers.

**Resources and levers:** The power to set agendas, expose misconduct (much of what is known about OpenAI's 2023 crisis, and about its practice of clawing back critics' equity, came from reporting), and frame debates.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Companies that control access and leak selectively. Shrinking newsrooms. AI products that siphon off the search traffic news sites depend on.
