**What it is:** A Hangzhou lab funded by the quantitative hedge fund High-Flyer and led by its founder, Liang Wenfeng. Its R1 reasoning model shocked markets in January 2025 by matching top US models at a fraction of the reported cost; Nvidia lost nearly $600 billion in market value in a day.

**What it wants:** Liang has said the goal is AGI through fundamental research, with models released openly. The lab keeps a low profile and shows little interest in commercial products beyond its app and API.

**Resources and levers:** Strong engineering for doing more with less compute. Open-weight releases under permissive licenses that developers worldwide build on. Its April 2026 [V4 models](https://qz.com/deepseek-v4-model-huawei-chips-open-source-042426) run on Huawei's Ascend chips, a milestone for China's chip independence. Prestige at home that gives it access to state support.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Limited access to top Nvidia chips. Bans on its app on government devices in the US and several other countries. US evaluations, including a 2025 CAISI report, finding it lags US models and is more susceptible to misuse. Competition from Alibaba, ByteDance and the "AI tigers" at home.
