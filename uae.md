**What it is:** The United Arab Emirates' AI push, led by Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed, the national security adviser, who chairs the AI firm G42 and the investment fund MGX.

**What it wants:** To turn oil wealth into a durable place in the AI economy as a hub for data centers, investment and models, and to be a trusted US partner while keeping ties across the world. The UAE is set to host the international AI summit in 2028.

**Resources and levers:** Sovereign wealth on a scale few countries can match, cheap energy and land, and stakes in OpenAI, xAI and Stargate through MGX. A May 2025 deal with the US for a 5-gigawatt AI campus and large annual purchases of Nvidia chips; Stargate UAE is being built there.

**Obstacles and opponents:** US national security officials worried that chips or technology could leak to China; G42 cut ties with Chinese suppliers in 2024 to secure US partnerships. Members of Congress who question the security terms of the chip deals. Saudi Arabia, competing for the same role.
