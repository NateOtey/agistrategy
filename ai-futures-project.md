**What it is:** A small forecasting group led by Daniel Kokotajlo, who left OpenAI in 2024 and refused to sign a non-disparagement agreement at the risk of losing his equity. With Eli Lifland, Scott Alexander and others, it published [*AI 2027*](https://ai-2027.com), a month-by-month scenario of AI racing to superintelligence.

**What it wants:** To make the possibility of very fast AI progress concrete enough that governments and the public prepare for it. It updates its forecasts openly, including an [April 2026 revision](https://blog.aifutures.org/archive) of its timelines.

**Resources and levers:** A scenario read by millions, including the Vice President. Tabletop exercises with policymakers and labs. Kokotajlo's credibility as an insider who gave up money to speak freely.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Critics who see its timelines as alarmist or its scenario as science fiction. The risk that if predictions miss, the case for preparing loses credibility.
