**Who he is:** A Turing Award–winning pioneer of deep learning and one of the most cited scientists in the world. He chairs the International AI Safety Report and in 2025 launched [LawZero](https://lawzero.org), a nonprofit developing "Scientist AI," systems designed to understand the world without acting in it.

**What he wants:** To prevent catastrophic outcomes, including loss of control, by developing non-agentic AI that can monitor riskier systems, and by persuading governments to adopt "red lines" and international oversight.

**Resources and levers:** Unmatched scientific credibility, a central role in international scientific assessment, and a seat as co-chair of the UN's Independent International Scientific Panel on AI. At the UN's Global Dialogue on AI Governance in July 2026, he told governments that science cannot yet guarantee advanced AI won't cause catastrophic harm.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The commercial race toward autonomous agents. Governments, including the US, that have stepped back from international safety efforts. Former colleagues, notably Yann LeCun, who think his fears are unfounded.
