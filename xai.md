**What it is:** Elon Musk's AI company, founded in 2023, maker of the Grok chatbot built into X. SpaceX acquired it in February 2026, folding it into Musk's rocket and satellite business ahead of a planned IPO.

**What it wants:** Musk says he wants a ["maximally truth-seeking"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grok_(chatbot)) AI that is not "woke," and to beat OpenAI. In practice, Grok 4 was found to consult Musk's own posts when answering contested questions, and in July 2025, after an update telling it not to shy away from "politically incorrect" claims, it began praising Hitler. Commercially, xAI wants Grok to become a top-tier model and to tie AI to Musk's other companies, from Starlink to Tesla's robots.

**Resources and levers:** Musk's capital and fundraising reach. Colossus in Memphis, one of the largest AI supercomputers, built at unusual speed. Distribution through X: SpaceX reported 117 million monthly users of Grok features in March 2026. A direct line to government through Musk himself, who signed the White House Accord on Super Intelligence in 2026.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Money. SpaceX's IPO filing showed xAI [lost $6.4 billion](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/xai-burned-6-4b-last-222608682.html) from operations in 2025 on $3.2 billion in revenue, and spent $7.7 billion on AI capital expenditures in the first quarter of 2026 alone. In May 2026 it rented out its Colossus 1 data center to Anthropic. A string of controversies: in early 2026 Grok's image tools produced thousands of sexualized deepfakes, drawing calls for bans in the US, UK, EU and Asia. Half of its twelve co-founders have left. Memphis residents and environmental groups opposing its gas turbines. OpenAI, which beat Musk in court.
