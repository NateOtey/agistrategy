**Who he is:** Host of the [*Dwarkesh Podcast*](https://www.dwarkesh.com), known for long, technical interviews with lab leaders and researchers, from Ilya Sutskever and Dario Amodei to Mark Zuckerberg.

**What he wants:** To understand where AI is going and ask the questions others don't. He has defended both longer and shorter timelines than his guests. His widely read essay on the 2026 Hugging Face incident described OpenAI's cooperating agents as ["civilizations"](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/dwarkesh-patel-calls-openai-bots-civilizations-after-hugging-face-hack/tldr).

**Resources and levers:** An audience that includes much of the AI industry and policy world, and access to people who rarely give long interviews. Conversations on his show often become reference points in debates.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Few opponents as such. Like any interviewer, his access depends on guests choosing to appear.
