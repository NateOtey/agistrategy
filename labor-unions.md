**Who they are:** Unions representing workers whose jobs AI could change or replace, from SAG-AFTRA and the Writers Guild in entertainment to the Teamsters and the AFL-CIO.

**What they want:** A say in how AI is used at work, protection against job losses, consent and pay when workers' voices, faces or writing are used to train AI, and a share of the gains. SAG-AFTRA and the Writers Guild won AI protections in their 2023 strikes, and video game actors struck over AI in 2024 and 2025.

**Resources and levers:** Contracts and strikes, political endorsements and turnout, and lobbying for state laws such as requiring human operators in autonomous trucks.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Employers looking to cut costs, AI companies, and a political environment focused on competitiveness. Falling union membership in much of the private sector.
