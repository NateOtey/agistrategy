**What it is:** The UK hosted the first AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park in 2023 and created the first government AI Safety Institute, renamed the [AI Security Institute](https://www.aisi.gov.uk) in February 2025. It is the best-funded government AI evaluation team in the world.

**What it wants:** To be the bridge between the US and Europe on AI, attract AI investment, and give government technical understanding of frontier risks, especially security risks. The government has repeatedly delayed a promised frontier AI bill to avoid friction with Washington and the industry.

**Resources and levers:** The AI Security Institute's technical staff, who test models before release by agreement with the labs and publish research. Diplomatic weight and research universities. A 2025 "Tech Prosperity Deal" with the US.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Little leverage over US companies, which are not obliged to cooperate. Limited compute. Pressure from Washington against regulation.
