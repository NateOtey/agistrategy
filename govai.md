**What it is:** The [Centre for the Governance of AI](https://www.governance.ai), founded at Oxford and now an independent nonprofit. It is one of the main research hubs for AI governance and a training ground for policy staff who now work in labs and governments.

**What it wants:** Better institutions for governing advanced AI: compute governance, frontier regulation and international coordination.

**Resources and levers:** Influential research, fellowships that have trained many of the field's policy people, and alumni across labs, think tanks and governments.

**Obstacles and opponents:** A shrinking appetite for regulation in Washington. Critics who see AI governance as an extension of effective altruism.
