**Who he is:** A California state senator from San Francisco and author of the state's two major frontier AI bills. In 2026 he is running for Nancy Pelosi's House seat; he [advanced](https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/06/05/scott-wiener-d-and-connie-chan-d-advanced-from-the-top-two-primary-in-californias-11th-congressional-district-on-june-2-2026/) from the June primary to a November runoff against Connie Chan, another Democrat.

**What he wants:** Basic safety rules for the largest AI developers. His [SB 1047](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safe_and_Secure_Innovation_for_Frontier_Artificial_Intelligence_Models_Act) (2024), which would have made developers liable for catastrophic harms, was vetoed. Its successor, SB 53, signed in 2025, requires frontier developers to publish safety frameworks, report serious incidents and protect whistleblowers.

**Resources and levers:** Expertise in drafting AI law, relationships with labs and safety groups, and a model bill other states have copied. A seat in Congress would carry the fight to the federal level.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Much of the tech industry, including Andreessen Horowitz, which fought SB 1047. Federal preemption efforts that could wipe out SB 53.
