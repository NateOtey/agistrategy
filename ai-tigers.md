**Who they are:** China's leading AI startups, chiefly Moonshot AI (maker of the Kimi models), Zhipu AI (Z.ai) and MiniMax. Zhipu and MiniMax [listed in Hong Kong](https://restofworld.org/2026/zhipu-ai-minimax-ipo/) in early 2026. Since January 2025 Zhipu has been on the US Entity List, the Commerce Department's blacklist of foreign companies that American firms need a special license to sell to.

**What they want:** To stay relevant against China's tech giants by releasing strong open-weight models, especially for coding and agents, and to turn that into revenue at home and abroad.

**Resources and levers:** Fast release cycles, low prices, backing from Alibaba, Tencent and state-linked funds, and new public-market capital. Models like Kimi K2 have rivaled US frontier models on some benchmarks.

**Obstacles and opponents:** ByteDance, Alibaba and DeepSeek. US export controls. Thin margins in a market where open models are free.
