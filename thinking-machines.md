**What it is:** A startup founded in February 2025 by Mira Murati, OpenAI's former CTO, with a group of senior OpenAI researchers. It raised a $2 billion seed round at a $12 billion valuation, the largest seed round on record.

**What it wants:** AI that collaborates with people instead of replacing them, more open research than the big labs, and, in Murati's [recent public remarks](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/04/mira-murati-steps-back-into-the-spotlight-carefully/), less concentration of consequential AI decisions in a few hands. Like every new lab, it also needs a product that justifies its valuation.

**Resources and levers:** Murati's standing and network, a team drawn from OpenAI's core, and investors including Nvidia and Andreessen Horowitz. Its first product, Tinker, helps researchers fine-tune open models.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The same capital race SSI faces, with little revenue to show for it so far. A series of departures, including co-founder Andrew Tulloch to Meta in late 2025. Competition for talent from labs that can pay far more.
