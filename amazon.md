**What it is:** The world's largest cloud provider through AWS. It invested heavily in Anthropic and built Project Rainier, a giant cluster of its own Trainium chips, largely for Anthropic. In February 2026 it also committed [up to $50 billion](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazon-invests-50b-in-openai-deepens-aws-partnership-with-expanded-100b-cloud-deal/) to OpenAI.

**What it wants:** For AWS to stay the default place to build and run AI, and to reduce its reliance on Nvidia by getting top labs onto Amazon's own chips. Backing both leading independent labs keeps it close to the frontier without building one itself.

**Resources and levers:** The largest cloud business, its custom chips, enormous capital spending, and the ability to tie investments to cloud contracts: OpenAI's deal included a $100 billion expansion of its AWS spending, and much of Amazon's money in Anthropic is expected to come back as payments for Trainium.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The circularity of those deals: Amazon's investments return as cloud revenue only if both labs keep growing and keep raising money. Microsoft and Google in cloud, both with stronger in-house models. Nvidia's dominance, which its chips have yet to break. Power and permitting for new data centers. The awkwardness of funding two labs that compete with each other.
