**What it is:** A San Francisco nonprofit led by Dan Hendrycks, best known for its 2023 [one-sentence statement](https://www.safe.ai/work/statement-on-ai-risk) that "mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority," signed by the heads of OpenAI, Google DeepMind and Anthropic. Hendrycks also advises xAI and Scale AI.

**What it wants:** To reduce catastrophic risks through research, benchmarks and policy. With Eric Schmidt and Alexandr Wang, Hendrycks co-wrote ["Superintelligence Strategy"](https://www.nationalsecurity.ai) (2025), arguing that the threat of sabotage between rival powers ("mutual assured AI malfunction") could deter any one country from racing to superintelligence.

**Resources and levers:** Widely used benchmarks such as Humanity's Last Exam, a compute cluster for safety researchers, a lobbying arm (CAIS Action Fund) that co-sponsored California's SB 1047, and connections across labs and governments.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Tech-industry groups that opposed SB 1047. Critics who question its ties to industry. Governments uninterested in deterrence frameworks.
