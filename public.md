**Who they are:** Everyone else. Hundreds of millions of people use AI products, and most have opinions about AI even if they rarely vote on it.

**What they want:** Polls show broad support for AI regulation and more concern than excitement. In 2025 surveys, majorities of Americans said they wanted safety rules even if they slowed development, and worried most about jobs, misinformation and children.

**Resources and levers:** Votes, adoption (what people use determines which companies win), juries in lawsuits, and local politics, as data-center fights show.

**Obstacles and opponents:** AI ranks low among most voters' priorities. Information about AI is confusing and contested. Companies and political groups spending to shape opinion.
