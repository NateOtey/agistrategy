**What it is:** [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com), the forum Eliezer Yudkowsky founded in 2009 that became the home of the rationalist community and much early writing on AI risk. Since 2017 it has been run by Lightcone Infrastructure, led by Oliver Habryka, which also runs the [AI Alignment Forum](https://www.alignmentforum.org) and Lighthaven, a conference campus in Berkeley.

**What it wants:** Better reasoning about the most important problems, above all making AI go well, and a place where researchers can work through hard ideas in public.

**Resources and levers:** Agenda-setting. Many ideas that later shaped lab safety teams and policy debates (inner alignment, AI control, scheming, *AI 2027*'s scenario) were first posted, argued over and refined there, and lab researchers still publish and debate on the Alignment Forum. Lighthaven hosts many of the field's conferences and workshops. The forum is also a recruiting ground: a large share of AI safety researchers are, or once were, regular readers.

**Obstacles and opponents:** A reputation, among critics on both left and right, as an insular subculture whose norms put off outsiders. Its fundraising since 2024, when it has had to raise much of its budget from readers and individual donors. Competition for attention from Substack, X and podcasts, where much AI discussion has moved.
