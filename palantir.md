**What it is:** A data-analytics and AI software company co-founded by Peter Thiel and led by CEO Alex Karp, and one of the US government's most important tech contractors. In 2025 the Army signed an enterprise agreement with it worth up to $10 billion.

**What it wants:** For the West to win the AI era militarily and economically, with Silicon Valley serving national defense. Karp's 2025 book *The Technological Republic* argues that tech companies have a duty to work with the government.

**Resources and levers:** Deep integration in defense and intelligence agencies, platforms that put AI models to work on government data, and a market value that makes it one of the largest defense companies.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Civil liberties advocates who criticize its work for immigration enforcement and surveillance. Competing contractors. Employees and critics opposed to military AI.
