**Who he is:** CEO of Tesla and SpaceX (which now includes xAI), owner of X, and the world's richest person. He co-founded OpenAI in 2015, left in 2018, and has been its loudest critic since.

**What he wants:** Musk has warned about AI risk for over a decade, calling it potentially "more dangerous than nukes," and he supported California's SB 1047 in 2024. In September 2026 he responded to Dario Amodei's call to pace the frontier with "Dario is right." He also wants his own companies to win the AI race and believes rival AIs are being trained toward the wrong values. He frames both goals as one: if superintelligence is coming, it should be built by a company that is "maximally truth-seeking," a standard that in practice has tracked his own opinions.

**Resources and levers:** Wealth in the hundreds of billions. X, which gives him one of the largest megaphones in politics. SpaceX's satellites and launch capacity. Influence in Washington, though uneven: he led the DOGE cost-cutting effort in 2025 before a public falling-out with Trump, and was back at the White House to sign the Accord on Super Intelligence in 2026.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Sam Altman and OpenAI; a jury took under two hours to [dismiss his lawsuit](https://tollbit.time.com/article/2026/05/19/elon-musk-openai-trial-xai-jury-verdict/) in May 2026. xAI's losses. Regulators in Europe and elsewhere over content on X and Grok. The limits of his own attention across several companies, and a record of departures at xAI.
