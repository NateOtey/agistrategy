**What it is:** The Defense Department, which the administration also calls the Department of War, under Secretary Pete Hegseth. In 2025 it gave contracts worth up to $200 million each to Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and xAI.

**What it wants:** To use frontier AI across the military quickly and on its own terms, for "all lawful purposes," without vendors setting limits on how it uses their products.

**Resources and levers:** The largest procurement budget in the world. The power to label companies "supply chain risks," which it used against Anthropic in February 2026 after Anthropic refused to allow mass domestic surveillance or fully autonomous weapons. Contracts as a reward: OpenAI announced its own Pentagon deal hours later. A seat on the new Super Intelligence Force through its chief technology officer, Emil Michael.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Anthropic, which sued. The courts have split: in August 2026 a district judge [ruled](https://www.wamc.org/2026-08-28/judge-says-pentagons-measures-against-anthropic-were-illegal-and-baseless) that the department's measures were illegal retaliation for Anthropic's criticism, but on September 25 the D.C. Circuit [sided with the Pentagon](https://broadbandbreakfast.com/federal-court-says-pentagon-can-label-anthropic-a-supply-chain-risk/) 2–1 on the designation itself, letting it keep Claude out of defense work while the fight continues. Civil liberties groups and critics of autonomous weapons. The slow pace of its own procurement.
