**Who he is:** PayPal and Palantir co-founder, early Facebook investor, and head of Founders Fund. He was an early funder of AI safety research (including MIRI) and is a longtime patron of JD Vance.

**What he wants:** An end to what he calls technological "stagnation," with breakthroughs in AI, energy and biotech. In a 2025 lecture series on the Antichrist, he argued that calls for global governance of AI and other technologies risk ushering in a totalitarian world state. He wants the West to lead in defense technology.

**Resources and levers:** Wealth, a network of protégés across tech and government, and investments in Palantir, Anduril and other defense-tech firms. Intellectual influence on the tech right.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Advocates of international AI treaties and global governance, from MIRI to the UN. Critics of surveillance technology. Parts of the populist right that distrust tech billionaires.
