**Who he is:** A former Google design ethicist who co-founded the [Center for Humane Technology](https://www.humanetech.com) and became known through the documentary *The Social Dilemma* (2020). His 2023 talk "The AI Dilemma" reached millions.

**What he wants:** To avoid repeating the mistakes of social media, where business models built on engagement harmed users, especially children. He wants regulation and liability to change AI companies' incentives.

**Resources and levers:** Media reach, a talent for making abstract risks vivid, and work supporting lawsuits and legislation on chatbot harms to young people.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Companies that profit from engagement. Critics who think his framing is alarmist or that analogies to social media don't fit.
