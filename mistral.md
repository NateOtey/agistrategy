**What it is:** A Paris-based lab founded in 2023 by former DeepMind and Meta researchers, led by CEO Arthur Mensch. It is Europe's best-funded frontier lab, and in 2025 the Dutch chip-equipment giant ASML led a €1.7 billion round in it.

**What it wants:** European "AI sovereignty": a homegrown alternative so that European governments and companies are not wholly dependent on American or Chinese models. It mostly releases open-weight models and sells to enterprises and governments that want control over their data.

**Resources and levers:** Political backing in Paris and Brussels, where it is treated as a national champion. Strategic investors like ASML and Nvidia. Influence over EU policy: it lobbied successfully to soften parts of the AI Act in 2023.

**Obstacles and opponents:** A large gap in compute and capital relative to US labs. Chinese open-weight models, which compete for the same users at very low cost. US labs opening European data centers to address sovereignty worries directly.
