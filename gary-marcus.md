**Who he is:** An NYU emeritus professor of psychology and AI entrepreneur, and the most prominent skeptic of the claim that scaling current models will produce AGI. He testified alongside Sam Altman before the Senate in 2023.

**What he wants:** Better AI built on different methods, and strong regulation of today's systems, which he sees as unreliable and overhyped. His position is unusual: skeptical of near-term AGI, yet pro-regulation.

**Resources and levers:** A widely read [Substack](https://garymarcus.substack.com), a willingness to pick public fights, and a long record of predictions to cite.

**Obstacles and opponents:** AI companies and investors whose valuations depend on rapid progress. Accelerationists. Safety advocates who think he underestimates current systems.
