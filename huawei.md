**What it is:** China's national telecom and electronics champion. Its Ascend AI chips are China's main alternative to Nvidia. It has been on the US Entity List, which bars American firms from selling to it without a special license, since 2019.

**What it wants:** To make China independent of American AI chips and to sell full AI systems at home and to countries wary of US dependence. In 2025 it published an Ascend roadmap running through 2028.

**Resources and levers:** State backing, a captive domestic market as Beijing pushes firms to buy local chips, and system-level engineering: its CloudMatrix design links many weaker chips to compete with Nvidia's best. DeepSeek's adoption of Ascend for V4 was a major endorsement.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Limited access to advanced chipmaking tools, which caps how many leading chips China's fabs can produce. Shortages of high-bandwidth memory. US export controls and diplomatic pressure on third countries not to buy its chips. Nvidia, whenever Washington lets it sell in China.
