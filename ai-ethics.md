**Who they are:** Researchers who focus on AI's present-day harms. Gebru, fired from Google in 2020 after a dispute over a paper co-written with Bender, now leads the [DAIR Institute](https://www.dair-institute.org). Bender and Alex Hanna wrote *The AI Con* (2025).

**What they want:** Accountability for harms happening now (bias, surveillance, labor exploitation, misinformation) and less hype. They see "AGI" talk, from boosters and doomers alike, as a distraction that benefits the companies.

**Resources and levers:** Academic standing, influence on journalists, and ties to civil-rights and labor groups.

**Obstacles and opponents:** AI companies and their marketing. The existential-risk community, whose funding and focus they criticize. Accelerationists.
