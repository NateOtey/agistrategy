**What it is:** A series of government summits that began at Bletchley Park (2023) and continued in Seoul (2024), Paris (2025) and [New Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India_AI_Impact_Summit_2026) (2026). Geneva hosts in 2027 and the UAE in 2028.

**What it wants:** That depends on the host. Bletchley produced a shared declaration on frontier risks and the first AI Safety Institutes; Seoul produced voluntary safety commitments from 16 companies. Paris and New Delhi shifted toward investment, adoption and "impact," and the US and UK declined to sign the Paris declaration.

**Resources and levers:** Heads of state in one room, deadlines that force governments and companies to announce commitments, and the [International AI Safety Report](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org) led by Yoshua Bengio, commissioned at Bletchley.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Waning interest in safety among major governments; the US withheld support from the 2026 International AI Safety Report. Commitments that are voluntary and hard to verify.
