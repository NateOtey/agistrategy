**Who he is:** OpenAI's CEO since 2019 and the most recognizable face of the AI industry. OpenAI's nonprofit board [fired him](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Removal_of_Sam_Altman_from_OpenAI) in November 2023 for not being "consistently candid"; he was reinstated within five days after most of the staff threatened to quit.

**What he wants:** By his own account, to deliver AGI that makes the world radically richer, and to keep OpenAI in the lead while doing it. He has argued that the safest path is to release capable systems gradually so society can adapt. He wants abundant compute and energy, and has called for some government oversight of the most powerful systems while opposing rules he sees as slowing American companies. In September 2026 he publicly agreed "that we need to pace the frontier."

**Resources and levers:** Control of OpenAI's strategy and dealmaking, which has produced hundreds of billions of dollars in compute commitments. A wide network across Silicon Valley, Washington and the Gulf. Investments in adjacent bets like Helion (fusion) and World (identity verification). An unusual talent for holding very large coalitions of investors and partners together.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Elon Musk, his former co-founder turned rival. A trust deficit with parts of the safety community and some former colleagues, which the 2023 firing put on public record. Lawsuits and regulators. The financing gap at the center of his strategy: OpenAI's commitments far exceed its revenue, which leaves it exposed if investor appetite cools. He has ruled out an OpenAI IPO in 2026.
