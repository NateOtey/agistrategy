**Who they are:** Companies that supply the human-generated data used to train and test AI models, from labeled images to expert-written answers. Scale AI is the best known; Meta bought 49% of it in 2025. Rivals include Surge AI and Mercor.

**What they want:** To remain essential as models need more expert data, increasingly from doctors, lawyers and scientists, and to sell AI services to governments.

**Resources and levers:** Networks of hundreds of thousands of contractors, relationships with every major lab, and government contracts.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Labs building their own data pipelines or using AI-generated data. After the Meta deal, Google and OpenAI moved work away from Scale. Criticism over pay and conditions for data workers, many in lower-income countries.
