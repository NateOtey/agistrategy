**Definition:** Three related worries about who ends up in charge.

- **Extreme power concentration:** a small group, e.g. a company, a faction within a government, or a single leader, gaining unprecedented and unchecked control by means of advanced AI.
- **AI-enabled coup:** a small group using AI to seize power illegitimately, for instance by relying on AI systems loyal to them, AI systems with hidden loyalties, or AI that replaces the many people whose cooperation a power grab used to require.
- **Lock-in:** an arrangement of power or values becoming effectively permanent, since advanced AI could enforce it indefinitely without the turnover and dissent that eventually wear down human regimes.

**Example or analogy:** Every regime in history has depended on the cooperation of large numbers of people: soldiers, police, clerks, tax collectors. When enough of them refuse to go along, as when soldiers decline to fire on protesters, even entrenched regimes fall. AI could remove that check by replacing the people whose refusal once mattered. (Historically, the main obstacle to seizing absolute power has been the inconvenient need for other people to cooperate.)

**Relevance to strategy:** Power concentration is a risk even if AI is perfectly obedient; in fact, perfect obedience to the wrong person is the problem. It cuts across the usual divide between "misuse" and "misalignment," and it complicates the race framing, since winning a race could concentrate power in a few hands. Proposed defenses include requiring AI systems to follow the law over any individual's instructions, auditing models for hidden loyalties, publishing the rules models are trained to follow, distributing access to frontier capabilities, and preserving checks within both companies and governments.

**Where people disagree:** Which actors pose the greatest risk: AI companies, national governments, or foreign rivals. Strategies that guard against one can strengthen another; for example, nationalizing AI development might reduce corporate power while concentrating it in the state.
