**What it is:** A nonprofit, founded in 2011 and named after the hours in a typical career, that advises people on high-impact careers through articles, a long-running podcast, one-on-one advising and a job board. In 2025 it [narrowed its focus](https://80000hours.org/2025/04/strategic-approach) to helping the transition to advanced AI go well, saying "it's plausible that frontier AI companies will develop AGI within the next decade."

**What it wants:** More talented people working on AI safety, governance and security, in roles at labs, governments, think tanks and safety organizations.

**Resources and levers:** A pipeline. Its career guides, podcast and job board shape which jobs thousands of ambitious people apply for, and many staff now at labs, safety organizations and government AI offices first found those paths through it. Funding from Coefficient Giving and other donors in the same network.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Critics who see it as a recruiting arm for effective altruism, a label that has carried political baggage since the FTX collapse. The risk that it helps staff the very labs racing ahead, since it lists jobs at frontier companies. Competition for talent from labs that pay far more than the nonprofits and government offices it steers people toward.
