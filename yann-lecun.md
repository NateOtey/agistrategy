**Who he is:** A Turing Award–winning deep learning pioneer and Meta's chief AI scientist until late 2025, when he left to found AMI Labs, which [raised about $1 billion](https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/10/yann-lecuns-new-startup-ami-labs-raises-1-03b-train-world-models/) in March 2026 to build "world models."

**What he wants:** To show that large language models are a dead end on the road to human-level AI and that systems which learn from video and the physical world will get there. He considers existential-risk fears overblown and is a leading defender of open-source AI.

**Resources and levers:** Scientific credibility, a large social media following, and a new well-funded lab.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Hinton, Bengio and the safety community. Proponents of scaling language models, who keep producing better results. Labs and governments that want to restrict open models.
