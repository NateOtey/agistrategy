**What it is:** The [Machine Intelligence Research Institute](https://intelligence.org), founded by Eliezer Yudkowsky in 2000 and one of the oldest AI safety organizations. After concluding that technical alignment research was unlikely to succeed in time, it shifted to policy and public communication.

**What it wants:** An international treaty halting the development of superintelligence, enforced by controls on advanced chips and data centers, until humanity knows how to build it safely. Its 2025 book, [*If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies*](https://ifanyonebuildsit.com), made the case to a mass audience and became a *New York Times* bestseller.

**Resources and levers:** A clear, uncompromising message; Yudkowsky's influence on the field (many lab founders first encountered AI risk through his writing); and draft treaty language from its technical governance team.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Labs and governments committed to the race. Accelerationists, who see MIRI as the archetypal "doomer" group. Others in AI safety who think its predictions are too confident or its demands unachievable.
