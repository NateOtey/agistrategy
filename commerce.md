**What it is:** The Commerce Department under Secretary Howard Lutnick. Its Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) writes and enforces export controls on chips and AI technology and keeps the Entity List, a blacklist of foreign companies that American firms need a special license to sell to. Commerce also houses CAISI.

**What it wants:** To keep the most advanced US technology out of rivals' hands while expanding exports of American chips to partners, and to use export licenses as leverage in negotiations. It scrapped the Biden-era "AI diffusion rule" in May 2025, approved Nvidia chip sales to China in exchange for a share of revenue, and approved large chip sales to the UAE and Saudi Arabia.

**Resources and levers:** Export licenses for chips, chipmaking tools and, in some cases, models. In June 2026 its export controls led Anthropic to suspend access to its Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models until the restrictions were lifted on June 30. The Entity List.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Chip smugglers, who move restricted chips through third countries. Nvidia's lobbying against tighter rules. China hawks in Congress who want stricter controls. Limited enforcement staff.
