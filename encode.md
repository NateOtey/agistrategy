**What it is:** A youth-led AI policy nonprofit, formerly Encode Justice, founded in 2020 by Sneha Revanur when she was 15. With a staff of a few people, it helped pass California's SB 1047 through the legislature and then its successor, [SB 53](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/7orGteym2C83jRavk/sneha-revanur-on-how-a-small-team-of-activists-helped-pass), which became law in 2025.

**What it wants:** Binding safety and transparency rules for frontier AI developers, a voice for young people who will live longest with the consequences, and accountability for AI companies, including scrutiny of OpenAI's conversion to a for-profit company.

**Resources and levers:** Skill at drafting bills and building coalitions in state legislatures, a youth network that gives it moral standing, and allies among safety researchers and funders.

**Obstacles and opponents:** OpenAI, which in 2025 [subpoenaed](https://fortune.com/2025/10/10/a-3-person-policy-non-profit-that-worked-on-californias-ai-safety-law-is-publicly-accusing-openai-of-intimidation-tactics) Encode and its general counsel, Nathan Calvin, seeking private communications about SB 53 and implying it was funded by Elon Musk, which Encode denies. The industry super PACs. Federal preemption, which would undo the state laws it helped pass.
