**What it is:** OpenAI's earliest major backer and, after the 2025 restructuring, owner of roughly 27% of the company. Under CEO Satya Nadella it sells AI through Azure and Copilot, and since late 2025 it has its own "superintelligence" team led by Mustafa Suleyman.

**What it wants:** To be the platform where businesses use AI, whoever builds the best model. Its October 2025 deal with OpenAI secured access to OpenAI's technology into the 2030s while freeing Microsoft to pursue AGI itself. Suleyman has said he wants "humanist superintelligence" that stays under human control, and has argued against building AI that seems conscious.

**Resources and levers:** Azure's global data-center footprint, enterprise relationships with nearly every large company, Windows and Office distribution, and one of the largest cash flows on earth. Stakes in both OpenAI and, since late 2025, Anthropic.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Concentration risk: much of the value of its AI bet sits in its OpenAI stake, which depends on OpenAI raising hundreds of billions more. OpenAI itself, which has spread its compute across Oracle, Amazon and others. Google and Amazon in cloud. Power constraints for new data centers. Antitrust scrutiny of its AI partnerships.
