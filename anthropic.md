**What it is:** A lab founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers, including siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei, who left over safety and governance concerns. It makes the Claude models and is structured as a public benefit corporation overseen in part by a Long-Term Benefit Trust.

**What it wants:** To show that a lab can stay at the frontier while setting a higher bar for safety, and to use that position to shape industry norms and government policy. Its leadership argues that powerful AI is coming soon regardless, so it is better for safety-focused developers to be among those building it. In September 2026 it began pushing to ["pace the frontier"](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier): deliberately slowing capability gains so safety work can keep up.

**Resources and levers:** Frontier models with strong traction in business and coding, and a reported valuation near $1 trillion after a mid-2026 round. Compute from Amazon, Google, Microsoft and Nvidia, and since May 2026 from xAI's Colossus 1 data center. Its Responsible Scaling Policy, which other labs have imitated, and a unilateral commitment to give outside evaluators such as METR employee-like access, with the right to publish what they find. Political money: at least $20 million to the [Public First](https://letsdatascience.com/news/ai-super-pacs-duel-over-midterm-spending-894242e9) super PAC network in 2026.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The Department of War. After Anthropic refused to let Claude be used for mass domestic surveillance or fully autonomous weapons, the Pentagon designated it a "supply chain risk" in February 2026 and the President ordered agencies to stop using it. A district judge [ruled those measures illegal](https://www.wamc.org/2026-08-28/judge-says-pentagons-measures-against-anthropic-were-illegal-and-baseless) in August, but on September 25 the D.C. Circuit [ruled 2–1](https://broadbandbreakfast.com/federal-court-says-pentagon-can-label-anthropic-a-supply-chain-risk/) that the Pentagon can keep the designation and bar Claude from defense work. Its finances: it [reported](https://letsdatascience.com/blog/anthropic-first-operating-profit-q2-2026-559-million) a first quarterly operating profit in mid-2026, but has warned that planned compute spending could push it back into the red, and its May 2026 deal for Colossus 1 alone costs $1.25 billion a month through 2029. OpenAI and Google commercially. David Sacks and much of the tech right, who accuse it of seeking regulatory capture, and Nvidia's Jensen Huang, over export controls. Some safety advocates argue it accelerates the race it warns about.
