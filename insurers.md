**Who they are:** Insurance companies deciding whether and how to cover AI-related risks, from errors and lawsuits to cyberattacks. A small number of specialist firms now offer AI-specific policies.

**What they want:** To price risk accurately and avoid catastrophic losses. In late 2025, several large insurers sought permission to exclude AI-related liabilities from corporate policies because they could not estimate them.

**Resources and levers:** The power to make AI deployment expensive or impossible without safety measures. Insurance requirements can act as private regulation, as they have for building codes and car safety.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The lack of historical data on AI failures. The risk that a single flaw in a widely used model could cause many claims at once. Pressure from customers who want coverage.
