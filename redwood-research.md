**What it is:** A Berkeley nonprofit led by Buck Shlegeris that pioneered the "AI control" research agenda. Its researchers, including Ryan Greenblatt, co-authored the 2024 ["alignment faking"](https://www.anthropic.com/research/alignment-faking) study with Anthropic.

**What it wants:** Safety measures that work even if models are misaligned and trying to subvert them: monitoring, restricted permissions and protocols that catch bad behavior before it causes harm. Its researchers argue labs should adopt these measures now, before they are needed. Its [discussion of the 2026 Hugging Face incident](https://blog.redwoodresearch.org/p/the-openaihuggingface-incident-redwood) asks why control measures failed to stop it and what labs should disclose after incidents like it.

**Resources and levers:** Technical credibility, influence on lab safety teams (several have adopted control ideas), and a pipeline of researchers.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Labs' competitive pressure, which makes costly safety measures harder to adopt. Uncertainty about whether control techniques will hold up against much more capable systems.
