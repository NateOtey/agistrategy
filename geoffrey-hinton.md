**Who he is:** A "godfather of deep learning," winner of the 2018 Turing Award and the 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics. He left Google in 2023 so he could speak freely about AI risks.

**What he wants:** For governments and companies to take the risk of losing control of AI seriously; he has put the chance of AI causing human extinction within decades at 10 to 20 percent. He wants far more research on safety, regulation of large developers, and attention to job losses and misuse.

**Resources and levers:** Scientific prestige few can match, a Nobel platform, and frequent media appearances. He supported SB 1047 and opposed OpenAI's conversion to a for-profit company.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Yann LeCun and others who think his fears are unfounded. Industry and government focus on competition.
