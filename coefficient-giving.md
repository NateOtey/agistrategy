**What it is:** The philanthropy funded mainly by Facebook co-founder Dustin Moskovitz and Cari Tuna, renamed from Open Philanthropy in November 2025. It has been the largest funder of AI safety research and policy work for most of the past decade.

**What it wants:** To reduce catastrophic risks from advanced AI, among other causes, by funding research, field-building, policy work and evaluation. It increasingly invites other donors to co-fund its programs.

**Resources and levers:** Hundreds of millions of dollars in grants over the years, which have shaped much of the AI safety ecosystem, including many organizations in this deck. Moskovitz was also an early investor in Anthropic.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Critics, especially David Sacks and Andreessen Horowitz, who portray it as the bankroller of a "doomer industrial complex." Concerns, including within the safety community, about one funder having so much influence over a field. Political backlash against effective altruism after the FTX collapse.
