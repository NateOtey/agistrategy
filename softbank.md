**What it is:** The Japanese investment group run by founder Masayoshi Son, which owns chip designer Arm and has become one of OpenAI's largest investors.

**What it wants:** Son has said for years that "artificial superintelligence" is his life's mission, and he has bet the company on being at its center. SoftBank sold its entire Nvidia stake in late 2025 to fund more OpenAI investment, and put another $30 billion into OpenAI's 2026 round.

**Resources and levers:** Tens of billions of dollars in capital, Arm's chip designs, a large stake in OpenAI, and its role as a Stargate partner.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Debt and concentration risk: SoftBank's fortunes are now closely tied to OpenAI's, and it has borrowed heavily to fund those bets. A history of high-profile losses (WeWork) that makes markets nervous. Competition from other mega-investors for the same deals.
