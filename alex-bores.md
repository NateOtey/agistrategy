**Who he is:** A New York State assemblymember and former Palantir engineer who wrote New York's RAISE Act, a frontier AI safety law signed by Governor Kathy Hochul in late 2025.

**What he wants:** Transparency and safety requirements for the largest AI developers, and to show that elected officials can stand up to the industry's political money.

**Resources and levers:** Technical background, a law on the books in the country's financial capital, and national attention as a target of industry spending.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The Leading the Future super PAC network, whose Democratic arm spent more than $8 million against him in the 2026 Democratic primary for New York's 12th district, though safety-aligned groups [spent more than $20 million](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5936042-bores-lasher-ai-primary/amp) supporting him. He [lost that primary](https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/06/24/micah-lasher-d-defeated-alex-bores-d-george-conway-d-jack-schlossberg-d-and-four-other-candidates-in-the-democratic-primary-for-new-yorks-12th-congressional-district/) in June 2026 to Micah Lasher; how much the spending mattered is disputed.
