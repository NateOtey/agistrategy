**Who they are:** State-backed Russian propaganda networks that use AI to produce fake news sites, social media personas and comments at scale. OpenAI's regular threat reports have described disrupting Russian operations, alongside others linked to China.

**What they want:** To divide Western societies, sway elections and undermine support for Ukraine. Some networks also try to "groom" chatbots by flooding the web with propaganda that models may later repeat.

**Resources and levers:** Cheap AI-generated text, images and video, networks of fake accounts, and state media.

**Obstacles and opponents:** AI labs' threat-intelligence teams, platform trust and safety teams, and researchers who track these networks. So far, most documented AI-assisted influence operations have struggled to reach large authentic audiences.
