**Who they are:** South Korea's two memory giants. They make most of the world's high-bandwidth memory (HBM), the stacked memory chips that sit beside every top AI processor. SK Hynix leads; Samsung has been catching up.

**What they want:** To capture as much of the AI boom as possible through memory, which has become a bottleneck and a source of record profits, while protecting their factories in China from US restrictions.

**Resources and levers:** Control over a component AI chipmakers cannot work without. Pricing power during shortages, which pushed memory prices sharply up from late 2025. The backing of the Korean government.

**Obstacles and opponents:** US rules restricting their Chinese plants and sales of HBM to China. Chinese memory makers trying to catch up. The boom-and-bust cycles that have always defined the memory business.
