**Who they are:** Traffickers, front companies and intermediaries that move export-controlled Nvidia chips into China, often through Southeast Asia or the Middle East.

**What they want:** Profit from the gap between what restricted chips sell for in China and what they cost elsewhere, and, for their Chinese customers, compute that export controls are meant to deny.

**Resources and levers:** Shell companies, falsified paperwork, and the sheer volume of global chip shipments, which makes checks difficult. Remote access to chips in cloud data centers abroad, which can sidestep physical controls.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The Commerce Department's export enforcement office and federal prosecutors, who have charged several schemes involving tens of millions of dollars in chips since 2025. Proposals in Congress to require location verification on advanced chips, which Nvidia opposes.
