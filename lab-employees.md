**Who they are:** The researchers and engineers who build frontier AI, and the subset who speak out. In June 2024, current and former OpenAI and Google DeepMind employees signed a ["Right to Warn"](https://righttowarn.ai) letter calling for protections for those raising safety concerns.

**What they want:** Most want to build AI that goes well, and many have strong views about how their employers should behave. Whistleblowers want legal protection and channels to raise concerns without losing their careers or equity.

**Resources and levers:** Their labor, in a market where top researchers are scarce: they can quit, and mass threats to quit reversed Altman's firing in 2023. Leaks, open letters and testimony. California's SB 53 added whistleblower protections, and bipartisan federal bills have been introduced.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Non-disclosure agreements, equity at risk, and loyalty to colleagues. Companies that discourage public dissent.
