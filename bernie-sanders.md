**Who he is:** Independent senator from Vermont, ranking member of the Senate committee on health, education, labor and pensions, and the leading voice on AI and workers on the left.

**What he wants:** To make sure AI's gains go to workers and not only to shareholders. A 2025 report from his committee staff estimated AI and automation could replace around 100 million US jobs within a decade. He has proposed a shorter work week and taxes on automation, and in March 2026 he and Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez introduced a [bill for a moratorium on new AI data centers](https://www.sanders.senate.gov/press-releases/news-sanders-ocasio-cortez-announce-ai-data-center-moratorium-act).

**Resources and levers:** A national grassroots following, a large online audience, and the ability to make AI and jobs a central issue for Democrats ahead of 2028.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The tech industry and its donors. Republicans in control of Congress. Moderate Democrats wary of anti-tech positions.
