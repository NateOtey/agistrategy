**Who he is:** A psychiatrist and the writer behind [*Astral Codex Ten*](https://www.astralcodexten.com) (formerly *Slate Star Codex*), one of the most widely read blogs in tech and the rationalist community. He co-wrote [*AI 2027*](https://ai-2027.com) with Daniel Kokotajlo and the AI Futures Project.

**What he wants:** For AI to go well, and for people to think clearly about whether it will. In ["My AI Opinions"](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/my-ai-opinions) (June 2026) he put the chance of AI catastrophe at about 20% and gave AGI even odds by 2034. He "nervously" supports pause activism and thinks there is about a 40% chance the US and China agree to a well-designed pause before it is too late.

**Resources and levers:** A large, loyal readership that includes many people who now work at AI labs, in safety research and in policy; his essays, such as "Meditations on Moloch," supplied much of the field's vocabulary for coordination failures. A grants program (ACX Grants) that funds small projects. Credibility as a careful thinker who publishes his probabilities and updates them in public.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Accelerationists, and critics on the left who see the rationalist community as insular or as an extension of tech money. The limits of a blog's reach beyond its audience. His own preference for argument over activism, which makes him an influence on players more than a player in his own right.
