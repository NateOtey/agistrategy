**What it is:** Saudi Arabia's AI strategy, run through Humain, a company launched in 2025 by the Public Investment Fund and chaired by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman.

**What it wants:** To diversify the economy away from oil under Vision 2030 and become a top AI hub: data centers, Arabic-language models, and a large share of global compute.

**Resources and levers:** The Public Investment Fund's hundreds of billions of dollars, cheap energy, and US approval (November 2025) to buy advanced Nvidia chips. Partnerships with Nvidia, AMD, AWS and xAI.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The UAE, competing for the same deals. US concerns about technology transfer and human rights. A shortage of local AI talent. Lower oil prices, which squeeze the budget.
