**Who they are:** Hacking groups working for China's intelligence and military services, such as the Volt Typhoon and Salt Typhoon campaigns that penetrated US infrastructure and telecom networks. In November 2025 Anthropic [reported](https://www.anthropic.com/news/disrupting-AI-espionage) that a Chinese state-sponsored group it tracks as GTG-1002 had used Claude Code to automate most of an espionage campaign against about 30 targets.

**What they want:** Intelligence, footholds in critical infrastructure in case of conflict, and Western technology, potentially including frontier model weights and lab research.

**Resources and levers:** State budgets, large and skilled workforces, patience, and increasingly AI agents that can do much of the hacking work themselves. The option of recruiting insiders at labs and chip companies.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Lab security teams, which detected and banned the GTG-1002 accounts. The NSA, FBI and private cybersecurity firms. Export controls and security standards for model weights.
