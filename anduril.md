**What it is:** A defense company founded in 2017 by Palmer Luckey (who also founded Oculus) that builds autonomous drones, submarines and the Lattice software that coordinates them. It is building a large factory in Ohio.

**What it wants:** To modernize the US military with cheap, autonomous, mass-produced weapons, and to beat China in that race. It has partnered with OpenAI on counter-drone systems and with Meta on military headsets.

**Resources and levers:** Venture capital, a valuation above $30 billion, fast product cycles compared to traditional defense firms, and growing Pentagon contracts.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Campaigners against lethal autonomous weapons. Traditional defense contractors. The Pentagon's slow procurement.
