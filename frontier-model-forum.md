**What it is:** An industry body founded in 2023 by Anthropic, Google, Microsoft and OpenAI, later joined by Amazon and Meta. It publishes shared safety practices and runs an AI Safety Fund.

**What it wants:** Common standards for frontier safety (evaluations, threat models, security) that its members can agree on, and that governments can adopt or point to. Its members also prefer industry-shaped standards to rules written without them.

**Resources and levers:** Direct access to safety teams at the major labs, a convening role, technical reports, and a channel for labs to share information about threats without running afoul of antitrust law. Its role could grow if the industry self-regulatory organization [proposed](https://www.cfr.org/articles/five-ways-to-make-the-new-ai-task-force-a-success) around the 2026 White House Accord on Super Intelligence takes shape.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Critics who see industry self-regulation as a substitute for binding rules. Antitrust concerns about competitors coordinating. Competitive pressure among its own members, and notable absences from its membership.
