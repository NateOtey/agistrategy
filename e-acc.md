**Who they are:** Guillaume Verdon, a physicist and founder of the chip startup Extropic, and the online movement he co-founded under the pseudonym "Beff Jezos": [effective accelerationism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effective_accelerationism) (e/acc), which celebrates rapid technological progress.

**What they want:** Faster AI development with as few constraints as possible. The movement treats growth as a moral imperative and sees AI safety advocates ("decels") as a threat to progress.

**Resources and levers:** A large following on X, ties to venture capitalists (Marc Andreessen listed "Beff Jezos" as a patron saint of his manifesto), and memes that have shaped the tech right's vocabulary.

**Obstacles and opponents:** AI safety advocates, regulators, and the many people who find "accelerate everything" alarming.
