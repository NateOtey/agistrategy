**Who they are:** Individuals or groups who might use AI to plan attacks or build weapons, especially biological or chemical ones. This card covers a threat more than a known actor; no AI-enabled mass-casualty attack has yet occurred. The man who blew up a Cybertruck in Las Vegas in January 2025 had used ChatGPT to research explosives.

**What they want:** Mass casualties, attention and fear.

**Resources and levers:** AI models that can provide expert-level guidance, especially open-weight models whose safeguards can be removed. Since 2025, Anthropic and OpenAI have both said they can no longer rule out that their top models give significant help to novices trying to make biological weapons, and both have added stricter safeguards as a result.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Lab safeguards and classifiers, evaluations by CAISI and the UK AI Security Institute, controls on DNA synthesis and laboratory equipment, and law enforcement. The physical difficulty of making and deploying biological weapons, which AI may lower but not eliminate.
