**What it is:** A research institute, led by Jaime Sevilla, that tracks trends in AI: compute, data, algorithms, costs and capabilities.

**What it wants:** To give decision-makers accurate data about where AI is heading. It aims to stay neutral on what should be done, and its staff hold a wide range of views on risk.

**Resources and levers:** The most widely used public [datasets on AI trends](https://epoch.ai/data), benchmarks like FrontierMath, and credibility with policymakers and journalists across camps.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Labs' secrecy about training runs and costs. Controversy over its FrontierMath benchmark, which OpenAI funded without this being disclosed at first. In 2025 several of its researchers left to found Mechanize, a startup aiming to automate all work, which unsettled some safety-minded supporters.
