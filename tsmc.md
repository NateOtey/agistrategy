**What it is:** Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company, which makes nearly all of the world's most advanced AI chips for Nvidia, Apple, AMD, Google and others. Led by CEO C.C. Wei.

**What it wants:** To remain the indispensable foundry while staying out of geopolitical crossfire. It has committed $165 billion to factories in Arizona, partly to ease US pressure, while keeping its most advanced production in Taiwan.

**Resources and levers:** A manufacturing lead that rivals have struggled to close, and pricing power from that lead. Its importance to both the US and China, sometimes called Taiwan's "silicon shield."

**Obstacles and opponents:** The risk of a Chinese blockade or invasion of Taiwan. US tariff threats and demands to move more production to America. Intel and Samsung trying to catch up. Rules that bar it from making advanced chips for Chinese customers.
