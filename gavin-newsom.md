**Who he is:** Governor of California, home to most leading AI companies, in his final term (ending January 2027) and a likely 2028 presidential candidate.

**What he wants:** To position California as both the center of AI innovation and a responsible regulator. He vetoed SB 1047 in 2024 as too broad, then signed SB 53 in 2025 along with a law regulating companion chatbots for minors.

**Resources and levers:** The power to sign or veto laws in the state where frontier AI is built, and a national platform.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Federal preemption efforts and the Trump administration generally. Industry lobbying from one side and child-safety and AI safety advocates from the other, who criticized his vetoes.
