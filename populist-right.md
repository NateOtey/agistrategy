**Who they are:** The wing of the MAGA movement, led in media by Steve Bannon's *War Room*, that sees AI as a threat to jobs, families and human dignity, and Big Tech as an enemy.

**What they want:** Limits on AI companies, protection for workers, and no federal preemption of state laws. Bannon was among the conservatives who signed the Future of Life Institute's October 2025 [statement](https://superintelligence-statement.org) calling for a ban on developing superintelligence until it can be done safely.

**Resources and levers:** A large and loyal audience, influence with Republican voters and state legislators, and a record of turning that pressure on the tech right: populist opposition helped kill the 2025 state-law moratorium.

**Obstacles and opponents:** David Sacks, Marc Andreessen and the tech right within the administration. Republican leaders who see AI as an economic and national-security necessity.
