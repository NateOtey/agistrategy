**Who he is:** An Estonian programmer who co-founded Skype and has spent much of his fortune on reducing existential risk. He co-founded the Future of Life Institute and Cambridge's Centre for the Study of Existential Risk.

**What he wants:** To give humanity a better chance of surviving the transition to superintelligence. He has been openly pessimistic about the current race and supportive of a pause.

**Resources and levers:** Grants through the [Survival and Flourishing Fund](https://survivalandflourishing.fund), which distributes tens of millions of dollars a year. Early investments in DeepMind and Anthropic, made in part to gain influence over how they handled safety.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The labs he once backed, now racing ahead. Accelerationists. The limited reach of philanthropy against trillions in commercial investment.
