**Who he is:** Republican senator from Texas and chair of the Senate Commerce Committee, which oversees most tech legislation.

**What he wants:** A national, light-touch approach that stops states from writing their own AI rules, which he argues would hand the race to China. In 2025 he led a ten-year moratorium on state AI laws and proposed the SANDBOX Act, letting companies apply for waivers from federal rules.

**Resources and levers:** His committee gavel, which controls what AI bills move in the Senate, and close alignment with the White House and tech industry on preemption.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The Senate voted 99 to 1 to strip his moratorium from the 2025 budget bill after Republican governors, state legislators and Senator Marsha Blackburn turned against it. Democrats and child-safety advocates who want protections in any federal deal. More than fifty Republican state lawmakers who [wrote to Trump](https://techpolicy.press/trump-and-gop-lawmakers-push-for-new-national-ai-legislation) opposing preemption in 2026.
