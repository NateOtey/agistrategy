**Who he is:** Director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy and co-chair of the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology. A former Scale AI executive and US chief technology officer in Trump's first term.

**What he wants:** To carry out the AI Action Plan: deregulation, faster infrastructure, and the global spread of an "American AI stack." At the UN in 2025 he rejected centralized global AI governance in favor of US-led standards and partnerships.

**Resources and levers:** Coordination of federal science and technology policy, a hand in drafting executive orders and the national framework, and close ties to the President's tech advisers.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Advocates of international AI governance at the UN and in Europe. States passing their own AI laws. Limited budgets and staffing at the agencies that carry out the plan. A crowded field of White House AI officials, now including the Super Intelligence Force.
