**What it is:** China's e-commerce and cloud giant, led by CEO Eddie Wu. Its Qwen models are among the most downloaded open-weight models in the world, and many developers outside China build on them.

**What it wants:** To be the infrastructure layer of Chinese AI: the cloud that companies rent, the models they build on, and eventually the chips they run on. Wu has called AGI the company's main goal and pledged hundreds of billions of yuan for AI infrastructure.

**Resources and levers:** China's largest cloud business, a huge open-model ecosystem, investments in startups such as Moonshot, and an in-house chip unit.

**Obstacles and opponents:** ByteDance, whose Doubao is China's most used chatbot, along with Tencent and DeepSeek. US chip controls. Scrutiny in the US over whether Chinese open models carry security risks for companies that adopt them.
