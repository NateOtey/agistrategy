**What it is:** A lab founded in June 2024 by Ilya Sutskever, OpenAI's former chief scientist, who voted to fire Altman in 2023 and then helped bring him back. Sutskever became CEO after co-founder Daniel Gross left for Meta in 2025.

**What it wants:** A "straight shot" to safe superintelligence with no intermediate products, so that commercial pressure never forces safety compromises. Sutskever has argued that the era of simply scaling up is ending and the field is back in an "age of research," which plays to a small lab with new ideas.

**Resources and levers:** Sutskever's reputation as one of the most important researchers in the field. Billions in funding at a $32 billion valuation (April 2025). Access to Google's TPUs. A small team insulated from product deadlines.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The capital race: rivals spend more on compute in a month than SSI has raised in total. No revenue, and little public evidence of progress, so its survival depends on investors' faith in Sutskever. The loss of its co-founder to Meta. The risk that if scaling keeps working, a research-first strategy falls behind.
