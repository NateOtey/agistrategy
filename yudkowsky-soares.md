**Who they are:** MIRI's founder and its president, authors of [*If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies*](https://ifanyonebuildsit.com) (2025). Yudkowsky has written about AI risk since the early 2000s and influenced many of the people who later founded labs.

**What they want:** An enforceable international halt to the development of superintelligence. In a 2023 [*TIME* essay](https://time.com/6266923/ai-eliezer-yudkowsky-open-letter-not-enough/), Yudkowsky argued that governments should be willing to enforce such a halt even against rogue data centers.

**Resources and levers:** A bestselling book, a devoted readership, and arguments that have shaped the field for decades.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The entire industry and most governments. Accelerationists. Other safety advocates who think their pessimism is unwarranted or counterproductive.
