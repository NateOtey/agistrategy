**Who he is:** Former Google CEO, chair of the National Security Commission on AI (2019 to 2021), and founder of the Special Competitive Studies Project and the philanthropy Schmidt Sciences.

**What he wants:** For the US to beat China in AI while managing the risks of the most dangerous systems. He co-authored ["Superintelligence Strategy"](https://www.nationalsecurity.ai) with Dan Hendrycks and Alexandr Wang. Schmidt Sciences funds AI safety science and AI for research.

**Resources and levers:** Wealth, decades of relationships in Washington and the Pentagon, think tanks that write policy proposals, and investments in defense technology.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Advocates of slowing down, who see his China framing as fuel for the race. Critics of his business interests in the defense and AI sectors he advises on.
