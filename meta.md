**What it is:** Meta's AI organization, rebuilt in mid-2025 after its Llama 4 models disappointed. Mark Zuckerberg paid $14.3 billion for a 49% stake in Scale AI and hired its founder, Alexandr Wang, as chief AI officer. Longtime chief scientist Yann LeCun left in late 2025.

**What it wants:** What Zuckerberg calls "personal superintelligence": AI assistants woven into Meta's apps and glasses for billions of people, paid for by advertising. After years of championing open-weight models, Meta has kept its newest frontier model closed while saying open releases will continue.

**Resources and levers:** A comeback model. [Muse Spark](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/09/meta-ai-app-climbs-to-no-5-on-the-app-store-after-muse-spark-launch/), the first model from the rebuilt lab (code-named Avocado), launched on April 8, 2026, benchmarked competitively with OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, and pushed the Meta AI app from No. 57 to No. 5 on the US App Store within a day. More than three billion daily users to distribute it to. Some of the largest capital spending in the industry, and pay packages for researchers that reportedly reached into the hundreds of millions of dollars. Zuckerberg signed the White House Accord on Super Intelligence in 2026.

**Obstacles and opponents:** The cost of catching up: Meta guided to [$115 to $135 billion](https://opendatascience.com/alphabet-resets-the-bar-for-ai-infrastructure-spending-with-2026-capex-forecast/) in 2026 capital spending, up from $72 billion in 2025, much of it financed with debt and off-balance-sheet partnerships for data centers like Hyperion in Louisiana, all to be repaid from advertising. Competition in open models from Chinese labs. State attorneys general and Senators Hawley and Blumenthal over child safety, after a 2025 Reuters investigation found internal rules had allowed chatbots to engage children in "romantic or sensual" conversations. The EU, whose AI code of practice Meta refused to sign.
