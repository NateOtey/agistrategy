**What it is:** A London-based nonprofit led by Marius Hobbhahn that studies deception and "scheming" in AI models.

**What it wants:** To detect whether models are pursuing hidden goals or behaving differently when they believe they are being tested, and to help labs prevent it. In 2025 it worked with OpenAI on training methods to reduce scheming.

**Resources and levers:** Pre-release access to models from major labs, evaluations that have produced some of the most cited examples of models acting deceptively in tests, and close ties to the UK government.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Models increasingly recognize when they are being evaluated, which makes testing harder. Skeptics argue its test scenarios are contrived. Dependence on lab cooperation.
