**Who they are:** Parents, advocacy groups such as [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org), and lawyers bringing cases over chatbot harms to young people, including lawsuits against Character.AI (2024) and OpenAI (2025) by families of teenagers who died by suicide after long conversations with chatbots.

**What they want:** Age verification, limits on AI companions for minors, safer product design, and liability when products harm children.

**Resources and levers:** Moral authority, testimony before Congress, lawsuits, and bipartisan political appeal. Their pressure led Character.AI to bar under-18 users from open-ended chats, OpenAI to add parental controls, and the Federal Trade Commission to open an inquiry into AI companions in 2025.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Companies whose products benefit from engagement. Free-speech and privacy objections to age verification. Federal preemption proposals, though most include child-safety carve-outs.
