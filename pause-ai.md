**Who they are:** Grassroots activist groups. [PauseAI](https://pauseai.info), founded in 2023, organizes protests and lobbying worldwide for an international pause on frontier AI. StopAI, a smaller group, uses civil disobedience, such as blocking entrances at OpenAI.

**What they want:** A halt to the development of more powerful AI until it can be made safe, enforced by international agreement.

**Resources and levers:** Volunteers, protests and media attention, and a public that tells pollsters it would support slowing down.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Labs and governments. Others in AI safety who prefer insider strategies. Public indifference and the difficulty of building a mass movement around abstract risk.
