**What it is:** A Dutch company with a monopoly on extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography machines, without which no one can make the most advanced chips.

**What it wants:** To keep selling its machines broadly, including older models to China, which was its largest market in 2024. It also wants Europe to have its own AI capacity, which is why it invested in Mistral.

**Resources and levers:** An irreplaceable product with years of lead time. Its role as a chokepoint makes it one of the strongest levers in export-control policy, even if the lever is pulled by the US and Dutch governments.

**Obstacles and opponents:** US pressure on the Netherlands to restrict sales to China. Chinese efforts to build domestic lithography tools. Revenue risk if its China business shrinks further.
