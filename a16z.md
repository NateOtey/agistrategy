**What it is:** One of Silicon Valley's largest venture firms, founded by Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz. Andreessen's 2023 ["Techno-Optimist Manifesto"](https://a16z.com/the-techno-optimist-manifesto/) named "trust and safety" and "tech ethics" among the enemies of progress.

**What it wants:** Fast, lightly regulated AI development, with room for startups ("Little Tech") to compete with incumbents. The firm opposes state AI safety laws and licensing regimes, defends open-source models, and wants a single federal standard that preempts the states.

**Resources and levers:** Billions in capital and stakes across the AI ecosystem, including OpenAI, xAI, Mistral and Thinking Machines. Political money: Andreessen and Horowitz each gave [$12.5 million](https://www.longtermwiki.com/knowledge-base/organizations/political-advocacy/leading-the-future) to the Leading the Future super PAC network. Alumni in government, including Sriram Krishnan, the White House's senior AI policy adviser. A large media operation of podcasts and newsletters.

**Obstacles and opponents:** AI safety advocates and the funders behind them, whom the firm portrays as a well-funded "doomer" lobby. State legislators like Scott Wiener and Alex Bores. Anthropic. Populist conservatives who distrust Big Tech, whichever size it comes in.
