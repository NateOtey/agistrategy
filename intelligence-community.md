**What it is:** The NSA, CIA and other US intelligence agencies, which both use AI and defend against adversaries using it. The NSA set up an AI Security Center in 2023. Since October 2026 the Director of National Intelligence, Jay Clayton, has also led the White House's Super Intelligence Force.

**What it wants:** To keep US models, model weights and lab secrets out of the hands of China, Russia and others; to track how adversaries use AI for hacking and influence operations; and to use AI for intelligence analysis.

**Resources and levers:** Classified intelligence on adversary programs, technical security expertise, the ability to brief labs about specific threats, and now a direct hand in AI policy through Clayton. Influence on how seriously Washington treats model security.

**Obstacles and opponents:** State hackers, above all from China and Russia. Labs whose security falls short of national-security standards, as the RAND framework for securing model weights made clear. Secrecy, which limits what it can share with the public and with labs.
