**What it is:** A nonprofit led by MIT physicist Max Tegmark, best known for its March 2023 [open letter](https://futureoflife.org/open-letter/pause-giant-ai-experiments/) calling for a six-month pause on giant AI experiments. It received a large endowment from Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin in 2021.

**What it wants:** To "keep the future human." In October 2025 it published a [statement](https://superintelligence-statement.org) calling for a prohibition on developing superintelligence until there is broad scientific consensus that it can be done safely and strong public support, signed by figures as varied as Geoffrey Hinton, Steve Bannon, Steve Wozniak and Prince Harry.

**Resources and levers:** Money for grants and campaigns, a talent for coalitions that cross political lines, the [AI Safety Index](https://futureoflife.org/index) grading labs' safety practices, and lobbying in Brussels and Washington.

**Obstacles and opponents:** Labs and accelerationists. The tech right, which treats it as a central "doomer" organization. Critics who say pause campaigns are unrealistic.
